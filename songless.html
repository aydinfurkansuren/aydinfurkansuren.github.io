Songless Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2, 2026

Songless ("the app", "we", "us") is a music-guessing game published by Aydin Furkan Suren. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and how it's handled. Songless is built to work without an account, a name, an email address, or a login — the sections below describe exactly what limited data it does use instead.

Information We Collect

Songless collects the following categories of data:

Data Purpose Stored where
Anonymous identifier On first launch, the app silently creates an anonymous Firebase Authentication identity tied to your install. There's no sign-up screen and it isn't linked to your name, email, or Apple ID. Firebase (Google)
Randomly generated display name A nickname (e.g. "SilverFox42") auto-generated on first play, used to identify you on the public leaderboard. You can change it in-app. Firebase Firestore
Game results & Elo rating Each round you finish is submitted to update your Elo rating and leaderboard rank. Firebase Firestore & Cloud Functions
Local game stats (streaks, win distribution) Tracks your personal play history for the in-app stats screen. On your device only (local storage), never transmitted
Purchase & subscription status If you buy Songless Pro, purchase and entitlement data is processed to unlock premium features and let you restore purchases. RevenueCat & Apple (App Store)
Device locale / storefront Used to fetch music charts and search results relevant to your country and language. Not stored — used per request

Songless does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location, and it does not require you to create an account.

The Leaderboard Is Public

Your display name, Elo rating, and games-played count are visible to other players on the global leaderboard. Your anonymous identifier, purchase history, and local stats are never shown to other players.

Third-Party Services

Songless relies on the following third parties, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Firebase (Google) — anonymous authentication, the leaderboard database, and the Cloud Functions that calculate Elo ratings. Google Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat — processes subscription purchases and entitlement status for Songless Pro. RevenueCat Privacy Policy
  • Apple (App Store & Apple Music/MusicKit) — handles in-app purchase transactions and provides the song catalog, chart, and preview data used in the game. Apple Privacy Policy

Data Retention

Leaderboard and rating data is kept for as long as your anonymous identity remains active. Local stats live only on your device and are removed if you delete the app. You can request deletion of your leaderboard data at any time — see Contact below.

Children's Privacy

Songless is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your Choices

  • You can change your display name at any time from within the app.
  • Deleting the app removes all locally stored stats.
  • To request deletion of your leaderboard/rating data, contact us using the details below.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
